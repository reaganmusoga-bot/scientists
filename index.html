What is SciPool UG

A national pool of young scientific talent

SciPool UG is the digital home of the Presidential Initiative for Young Scientists. It brings together students and early career researchers from every accredited Ugandan university into one collaborative ecosystem for science commercialisation, innovation and evidence based policy making.

Every year Uganda's universities produce hundreds of final year projects, research papers and working prototypes. Most never leave the campus shelf. They sit in hard drives, in drawers, in supervisors' offices and in students' memories. Meanwhile, Uganda imports the majority of its medical devices, industrial machinery, agricultural technology and digital systems. Most of these imports are conceptually no more advanced than what our own students already design.

SciPool UG exists to close that gap. It captures each innovation the moment it is finished, protects it with a timestamped hash, and puts it in front of the investors, manufacturers and government agencies who can turn it into a product, a business and a job.

        About SciPool UG

        SciPool UG is the national data, research and project repository for young scientific talents. It is the digital home of the Presidential Initiative for Young Scientists (PIYS), operating through the Inter University Young Scientists Consortium (IUYSC).

        It gathers the research of young and emerging scientists from every accredited Ugandan university, protects it, and connects it to industry, investors and government.

        Our five strategic pillars

        1. Discover. National innovation challenge and repository
        2. Develop. Incubation, scientific entrepreneurship, IP academy and prototyping
        3. Protect. National student patent program and IP support centre
        4. Commercialise. Start up accelerator, research marketplace and investment fund
        5. Industrialise. Manufacturing partnerships, industry linkage and export readiness
