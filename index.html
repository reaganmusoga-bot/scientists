What is PIYS?

A National Mobilisation of Young Scientific Talent

Built on the Inter-University Young Scientists Consortium (IUYSC), PIYS brings together students and early-career researchers from every accredited Ugandan university into a single collaborative ecosystem for science commercialisation, innovation, and evidence-based policy making.

🔬

Discover Talent

Identify, document, and celebrate research from Uganda's universities before it's lost to shelves and hard drives.

🛡️

Protect Innovation

Every submission is timestamped and SHA-256 hashed — a verifiable, legal-grade record of authorship.

🚀

Commercialise Research

Connect young innovators to investors, manufacturers, and industry partners who can scale their work.

🏭

Industrialise Uganda

Reduce dependence on imported technology. Build the industries of tomorrow from the inventions of today.

        About This Initiative

        The Presidential Initiative for Young Scientists (PIYS), operating through the Inter-University Young Scientists Consortium (IUYSC), is building Uganda's first national digital repository dedicated to protecting and advancing the research of young and emerging scientists.

        Our 5 Strategic Pillars

        1. Discover — National innovation challenge & repository
        2. Develop — Incubation, scientific entrepreneurship, IP academy, prototyping
        3. Protect — National student patent program, IP support centre
        4. Commercialise — Start-up accelerator, research marketplace, investment fund
        5. Industrialise — Manufacturing partnerships, industry linkage, export readiness
