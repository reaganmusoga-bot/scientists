About This Initiative

The Presidential Initiative for Young Scientists (PIYS), operating through the Inter-University Young Scientists Consortium (IUYSC), is building Uganda's first national digital repository dedicated to protecting and advancing the research of young and emerging scientists.

Every year, Ugandan universities produce hundreds of final-year projects and research papers that never leave the campus shelf. This repository exists to change that — capturing each innovation at the moment of completion, protecting it, and connecting it to industry, investors, and government.

Our 5 Strategic Pillars

  1. Discover — National innovation challenge & repository
  2. Develop — Incubation, scientific entrepreneurship, IP academy, prototyping
  3. Protect — National student patent program, IP support centre
  4. Commercialise — Start-up accelerator, research marketplace, investment fund
  5. Industrialise — Manufacturing partnerships, industry linkage, export readiness
